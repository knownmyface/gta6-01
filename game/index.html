HOW TO PLAY

Move / Drive: WASD or arrows · Look: click the game, then move the mouse
E: enter / exit car, bike, boat, shop, safehouse · F: punch
Click: shoot · Q: weapon wheel · 1 / 2: cycle weapon
R: change radio station · M: start / abandon mission · C: save game
Shift: sprint · Space: jump (on foot) / handbrake (in car) · P: pause
Goal: 22 missions — courier jobs, races, and the Golden Coral casino heist, ending in a boat getaway.

PAUSED

Press P or Esc to resume. Press C to save your game.
